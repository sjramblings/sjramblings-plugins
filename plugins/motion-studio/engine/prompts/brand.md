# Brand or product clip

```
Make a 20-second motion film for [PRODUCT] ([URL]) in films/<name>/, with the energy of
a motion designer's showreel.

Assets
- Open the site in a real browser. Save real screenshots, the real logo, real colours
  and fonts to films/<name>/assets/ and list what you found before you animate.
- Never redraw the product UI from imagination. Crop and animate the real thing.

Story (one beat each, 2 to 4 seconds)
1. Hook: the problem in five words of huge kinetic type.
2. The product appears; the UI assembles itself piece by piece.
3. Three features, each a UI moment with a cursor doing a real action.
4. One number that proves it works: [METRIC].
5. Logo lockup and [CTA].

Sound: 120 BPM, synthesised. Clicks and whooshes on the beat.
Formats: 9x16 first, then 4x5, 1x1 and 16x9 from the same timeline.
Before the full render, show me the contact sheet.
```

Keep one session per brand: the second clip is faster because the assets and style exist.
