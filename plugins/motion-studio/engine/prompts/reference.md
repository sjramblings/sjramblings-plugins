# Working from a reference

Without a reference the model falls back to centred text on a gradient. Naming a look
beats describing one. A frame, a video, or a folder of your own past work all count.

```
Reference: films/<name>/refs/launch.mp4 (or refs/*.png)

1. Extract one frame every 0.5s with ffmpeg into films/<name>/refs/frames/. Study them.
2. Write films/<name>/style_guide.md: palette (hex), type (family, weight, tracking),
   shot lengths, transition types, camera moves, texture, how text enters and exits.
3. Write films/<name>/shotlist.md for a [DURATION]s film about [SUBJECT] in that style.
   Take the grammar of the reference, never its content, logos or characters.
4. Show me both files. Wait for my OK before any scene code.
```

Say what to take (palette, type, pacing) and what not to take (subject). Specify the look
and the constraints, and leave the technique to the model.
